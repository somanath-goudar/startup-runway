Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Startup Runway Calculator is a free, client-side web tool. All calculations run entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Your financial inputs (cash, burn, MRR) are never transmitted anywhere.

Share Links

The share link encodes your inputs as a URL hash. This data never leaves your browser via our servers — it exists only in the URL you copy and share.

Third-Party Services

We load Chart.js and jsPDF from public CDNs (jsDelivr, Cloudflare). These CDNs may log your IP address as part of standard request logging.
